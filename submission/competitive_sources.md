# Competitive sources

**Checked 6 November 2026.** This is a capability comparison from official company pages, not an independent service-quality ranking or verified like-for-like price comparison. Product names, plan details and availability can change.

## JioBusiness

- Enterprise bundle page describes small-office packages that combine business internet with voice and work tools, alongside cloud-storage variants: https://www.jio.com/jcms/business/enterprise-bundle-plans/
- Business HSI page describes digital self-care, assisted service, Wi-Fi equipment and connectivity feasibility requirements: https://www.jio.com/business/services/connectivity/high-speed-internet/

## Tata Tele Business Services

- SmartOffice Business Broadband page describes broadband bundles with voice, endpoint security, DNS web protection and Microsoft productivity tools. The page exposes city, bandwidth and plan filters: https://www.tatatelebusiness.com/data-services/business-broadband/
- Smart Internet page describes leased-line service paired with DNS security, a DIY policy portal, visibility/reporting and managed support: https://www.tatatelebusiness.com/data-services/smart-internet/

## Airtel Business

- SME contact page describes internet, mobile, cloud telephony and collaboration offerings; tickets may go through the business portal or a service manager, while many plan changes are handled through an account or service manager: https://www.airtel.in/b2b/contact-us/
- Dedicated Internet page describes a needs study/site survey, tailored solutions and site-feasibility assessment: https://www.airtel.in/b2b/dedicated-internet

## Comparison boundary

The case solution should compete on a specific guided journey over Airtel’s existing portfolio. It must not claim that other providers lack bundles, support, self-service or digital products.
