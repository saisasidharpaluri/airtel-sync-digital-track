# Three-minute demo walkthrough

**0:00–0:20 — Problem and evidence**  
“Project Udyam Digital gives an MSME one guided route from needs discovery to activation tracking. In the supplied fictional quarter, 368 of 5,000 leads activated. The largest preventable losses include price objections, confusing or slow quotes, and KYC friction.”

**0:20–1:00 — Advisor and quote**  
Choose a tier-2 kirana with three employees, one site, no fibre, and no GSTIN. Show the wireless broadband recommendation and toggled billing add-on. Point out the starting monthly price, one-time setup, term, and why each product fits. Switch to a clinic profile to show a professional-office bundle.

**1:00–1:45 — Consent and KYC**  
Open Smart KYC and show that lookup is gated by consent. Select K04, choose its synthetic GST and Udyam specimens, and run the mock lookups. Then select K01 to demonstrate that a cancelled GSTIN routes to manual review. Call out that OCR is simulated and the sample data is fictional.

**1:45–2:10 — Tracker**  
Advance the order from site survey to installation booked. Show the owner-facing status and the partner handoff for a site check.

**2:10–2:40 — Impact and safeguards**  
Show the segment evidence and pilot metrics. Explain that churn is missing from the supplied data and that CAC baselines need reconciliation, so neither is presented as a measured improvement.

**2:40–3:00 — Close**  
“The pilot reduces avoidable effort first: explain the quote, complete mock-ready KYC, and replace status-chase calls with a tracker. Partners handle exceptions and site feasibility.”
