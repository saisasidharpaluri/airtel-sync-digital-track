## Narration

Project Udyam Digital is a guided onboarding journey for India’s small businesses. It uses the fictional Airtel SYNC case dataset. Across five thousand leads, 368 reached activation, a 7.4 percent rate. The opportunity is to make needs discovery, quote clarity, verification and follow-up easier for the owner and service team.

A retailer starts with business type, location, team size, fibre availability and GST status. The catalogue suggests eligible services, while the estimate shows monthly cost, one-time setup and minimum term together. Recommendations follow deterministic catalogue rules; this demo does not call external AI.

The segment data supports testing a focused beachhead. Professional offices activated at 11.1 percent and small multi-site firms at 11 percent, while micro retail and services were at 4.1 percent. Those are sample observations, not proof of causation. Keep a low-entry self-serve route for micro firms.

For a micro retailer, the example bundle is 1,797 rupees per month with 2,500 rupees setup. For a one-site clinic, the bundle is 3,171 rupees monthly with 2,000 rupees setup. These are starting catalogue prices. Taxes, coverage and installation feasibility still need confirmation.

Verification begins only after consent. The active match is a fictional registry result; the name-mismatch control routes to manual review. OCR uses specimen fixtures. No government registry or real business identity is checked, and uploads stay only in the browser session.

The prototype keeps the journey local: advisor, quote, demo KYC and order tracker. A missing, cancelled or mismatched record goes to a masked review and partner follow-up. The tracker gives the owner one place to check progress.

The market already offers business bundles, self-care and assisted support. JioBusiness, Tata Tele Business and Airtel Business each have established connectivity and service capabilities. This proposal does not claim rivals lack digital tools. It focuses on one guided entry path from product fit through follow-up.

The pilot target is 10 percent lead-to-activation over 90 days. At the observed five-thousand-lead volume, that implies 500 activations, 132 above the 368 baseline. If every incremental business used either the micro or single-site clinic bundle, gross list-price monthly run-rate would be 2.37 to 4.19 lakh rupees. This is a sensitivity, not a forecast, profit or return on investment.

Churn is missing, and the case CAC differs from the activated-row CSV average. Both need reconciliation. The pilot should measure activation, time, chase calls, product mix, margin, CAC and churn. Udyam Digital aims to reduce avoidable steps, then prove the value in a controlled pilot.
